# Product

register: brand

## What this is

The Web Suit is a limited-edition Spider-Man suit concept: 500 numbered physical units, each paired with a personal motion identity and serial portal by Digitivia. It is a mobile-first ecommerce experience, not a movie microsite, cast archive, or fan wiki.

## Users

Spider-Man fans who follow sneaker, fashion, gaming, and collectible drops. Most arrive from a social share on a phone, often at night, and need to understand the product, scarcity, price, and action in seconds.

## Purpose

The suit is the collectible object. Digitivia creates the digital world around ownership: an interactive product reveal, a unique motion signature, and a memorable post-purchase identity concept. This gives the collaboration a functional role beyond a visual credit.

## Strategic principles

- The supplied video opens the experience and carries the first viewport.
- The first screen states the product, price, scarcity, and reserve action.
- Mobile is the primary artboard. Touch, safe areas, short viewports, and native scrolling take priority.
- Use the supplied film only in the hero; every later visual is native vector motion.
- No GLB, WebGL, or model-viewer layer belongs in the experience.
- One product, one size run, one clear conversion path.
- Motion must reveal construction, hierarchy, or state. It cannot become a loading or interaction barrier.
- Digitivia appears as the digital collaboration layer and in the centered footer credit, never as a competing top-navigation brand.
- Keep the fan-made concept and no-payment disclaimer explicit.

## Anti-references

- Movie cast pages, trailer archives, and character timelines.
- Long WebGL loaders, sound-gated entry screens, and hover-only navigation.
- Comic-book kitsch, sci-fi dashboard panels, generic card grids, and corporate SaaS language.
- Visual effects that compromise video clarity, mobile performance, accessibility, or the reserve action.
